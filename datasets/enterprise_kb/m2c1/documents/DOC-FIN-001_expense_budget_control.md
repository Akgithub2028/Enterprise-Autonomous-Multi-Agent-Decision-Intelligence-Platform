# DOC-FIN-001 Expense Reimbursement and Budget Control Policy

Version: 1.0
Scope: Operational expense reimbursement, budget commitment, and overbudget approvals for Huaheng Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd.
Responsible Department: Finance Management Department; Department Heads and General Manager Office coordinate in execution.

## I. Expense Authenticity and Value Tiers

Expense reimbursement claims must possess genuine business purposes, compliant receipts/invoices, designated cost centers, and verifiable monetary values. The applicant, department approver, and finance reviewer are individually responsible for factual authenticity, business necessity, and regulatory compliance. Reimbursing personal expenditures, duplicate invoices, or non-business costs is prohibited, as is splitting a single transaction to downgrade approval tiers.

Clause ID: FIN-EXPENSE-SUBMISSION

Reimbursement expense reports must state occurrence dates, business purpose, transaction amounts, currency, project codes, budget accounts, participant lists, and receipt attachments. Multiple distinct expenses may be grouped within one claim provided each item remains auditable; installment payments for the same contract or event are evaluated based on the total financial commitment regardless of staggered billing.

Clause ID: FIN-EXPENSE-BELOW-5K

Single expense claims below 5,000 CNY are approved by the Department Head. A claim of 4,999 CNY falls within this tier; exactly 5,000 CNY does not. The Department Head verifies business authenticity, cost standard reasonableness, and budget availability; departmental approval does not waive invoice compliance, tax validation, or duplicate reimbursement checks by Finance.

Clause ID: FIN-EXPENSE-5K-20K

Single expense claims from 5,000 CNY up to (below) 20,000 CNY are approved by the Finance Manager. An 18,000 CNY expense claim falls within this tier. The requesting department must endorse the business purpose, but the formal value-tier approver is the Finance Manager; claims of exactly 5,000 CNY cannot be downgraded to Department Head approval, nor can claims below 20,000 CNY be erroneously escalated to the Finance Director.

Clause ID: FIN-EXPENSE-20K-PLUS

Single expense claims of 20,000 CNY and above require approval from the Finance Director. An amount of exactly 20,000 CNY enters this tier. Applications must attach supporting contracts, purchase orders, event documentation, and payment schedules; where procurement is involved, expense approvals do not substitute for procurement approvals in DOC-PROC-001, and both separate governance workflows must be satisfied.

## II. Budget Control and Overbudget Approvals

Clause ID: FIN-BUDGET-CHECK

Prior to submitting expense claims, active budget balances, committed expenditures, and projected remaining funds must be verified. The existence of available budget does not establish automatic expense justification; business necessity and spending guidelines must still be validated. Charging unrelated accounts or deferring entry of known commitments to fabricate available balances is prohibited.

Clause ID: FIN-OVERBUDGET-UPTO10

Budget overruns not exceeding ten percent require joint approval from both the Department Head and the Finance Manager. An overrun of eight percent falls within this clause, requiring both approvals without exception. The ratio is calculated with approved budget as the denominator and total projected excess spend as the numerator; calculating overruns against single claims or altering proportions across months is prohibited.

Clause ID: FIN-OVERBUDGET-OVER10

Budget overruns exceeding ten percent require joint approval from both the Finance Director and the General Manager. An overrun of exactly ten percent remains in the "up to 10%" tier; only overruns strictly exceeding ten percent enter this clause. Applications must justify root causes, alternative solutions, full-year financial impact, and ongoing control measures; business urgency cannot replace joint executive approval.

Clause ID: FIN-BUDGET-TRANSFER

Budget reallocations across departments or line items must be requested prior to expense incurrence, detailing donor account balances, recipient expenditure purposes, and operational impacts on the original plan, subject to Finance approval. Budget transfers cannot mask already incurred unapproved overruns or move restricted funds into broad accounts to evade oversight.

## III. Advance Payments and Disbursements

Clause ID: FIN-ADVANCE-PAYMENT

When advance cash or vendor prepayments are necessary, applications must specify recipient identity, contractual milestones, security guarantees, and settlement deadlines. Prepayment approvals must satisfy value-tier requirements, and formal receipts must be reconciled promptly upon task completion. Unreconciled personal advances or vendor prepayments past settlement deadlines trigger a hold on new advance requests unless justified by documented reasons verified by Finance.

Clause ID: FIN-EMERGENCY-EXPENSE

Urgent matters may request expedited processing but do not alter approval authorities for the 5,000 CNY, 20,000 CNY, or 10% budget overrun tiers. In the absence of timely approval, applicants cannot unilaterally commit company funds; in legal or safety emergencies requiring immediate action, evidence must be preserved and reported immediately to the Finance Director without establishing recurring post-hoc ratifications.

Clause ID: FIN-PAYMENT-RECONCILIATION

Prior to disbursement, Finance reconciles approval authorizations, contracts/orders, receiving acceptance, tax invoices, beneficiary legal entity, and payment amounts. Discrepancies require immediate hold and investigation; modifying beneficiary bank accounts requires independent verification. Completed payments are reconciled with bank statements and accounting ledgers to prevent duplicate payments across systems.

## IV. Audit and Anti-Splitting Controls

Clause ID: FIN-NO-SPLITTING

Splitting a single transaction across multiple expense reports, multi-person submissions, or staggered dates to evade approval thresholds is strictly prohibited. Finance identifies related transactions by vendor, timestamp, project code, and narrative summary; where distinct events occur, independent documentation must be provided. Evaded approvals must be re-evaluated under aggregate amounts with audit logging.

Clause ID: FIN-EXPENSE-AUDIT

Finance conducts periodic spot checks of high-value items, threshold-boundary values, recurring overruns, repeated vendors, and overdue advances. Revisions must preserve original vouchers, approvers, and modification trails. Fraudulent submissions or deliberate threshold evasion trigger payment freezes and referral to disciplinary governance, regardless of whether budget balances remain.

## V. Reporting and Governance

Monthly budget reports detail approved budgets, incurred spend, committed funds, full-year projections, overrun percentages, and approval statuses. Department Heads explain operational drivers, Finance Managers audit data integrity, and major overruns are reported by the Finance Director to the General Manager. All percentage boundaries are evaluated against raw calculated values without arbitrary rounding, distinguishing expense value tiers from budget overrun tiers.
