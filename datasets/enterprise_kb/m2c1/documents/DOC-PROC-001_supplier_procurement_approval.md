# DOC-PROC-001 Supplier Onboarding and Procurement Approval Policy

Version: 1.0
Scope: Supplier onboarding, standard procurement, and emergency procurement approvals for Huaheng Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd.
Responsible Department: Procurement Management Department; Supply Chain Center, Finance Management Department, and General Manager Office coordinate in execution.

## I. Supplier Onboarding

Purchase orders may be placed exclusively with vendors that have completed formal onboarding and maintain active status. Procurement Management is responsible for business identity, manufacturing capability, quality systems, and legal compliance documentation; requesting business units define technical specifications; Finance verifies payment accounts and tax registration. Xingyuan Power Components Co., Ltd., Ruihe Precision Electronics Co., Ltd., and Yuntong Supply Chain Services Co., Ltd. must maintain master records under identical procedures without exemption based on historical collaboration.

Clause ID: PROC-SUPPLIER-APPLICATION

Onboarding applications must detail stable supplier ID, supply category scope, corporate qualifications, quality management certifications, production capacity, delivery territories, settlement terms, and compliance declarations. Evidence must be verifiable; missing critical qualifications prohibits listing as an active vendor. Sample testing or price inquiries do not constitute onboarding approval or continuous purchase commitments.

Clause ID: PROC-SUPPLIER-DUE-DILIGENCE

Procurement Management conducts due diligence on conflicts of interest, major historical quality incidents, fulfillment track records, and information security posture. For vendors involving custom firmware, production business data, or L3 confidential documentation, the Information Security Office must evaluate data access boundaries. Identified risk items must have designated owners and closure criteria; unacceptable risks must be resolved prior to purchase order placement.

Clause ID: PROC-SUPPLIER-APPROVAL

Onboarding conclusions must explicitly indicate Approved, Conditionally Approved, or Rejected, recording authorized material categories, validity dates, and operational constraints. Conditionally Approved vendors may receive orders strictly within defined constraints; scope cannot be expanded before conditions are closed. Corporate name changes, ownership transfers, or consecutive performance failures trigger mandatory re-evaluation; reusing outdated approvals to mask vendor changes is prohibited.

## II. Standard Procurement Value Tiers

Clause ID: PROC-NORMAL-BELOW-50K

Standard purchase requisitions below 50,000 CNY are approved by the Department Manager. Total value is calculated based on single operational requirements, single vendor assignments, and aggregate commitments across reasonable consecutive periods, including indivisible supporting service fees. An amount of 49,000 CNY falls within this tier; exactly 50,000 CNY does not; requisitions require budget verification and vendor status validation.

Clause ID: PROC-NORMAL-50K-200K

Standard purchase requisitions from 50,000 CNY up to (below) 200,000 CNY are approved by the Procurement Director. An 180,000 CNY standard procurement falls within this tier and does not require joint executive approval for 200,000+ CNY, nor can it be downgraded to Department Manager approval. Applicants must submit competitive quotes or documented sole-source justifications with explicit delivery and quality criteria.

Clause ID: PROC-NORMAL-200K-PLUS

Standard purchase requisitions of 200,000 CNY and above require joint approval from both the Finance Director and the General Manager. Both approvals are mandatory; technical endorsement from the Procurement Director cannot substitute for joint executive authorization. Requisitions reaching exactly 200,000 CNY enter this tier; rounding or unbundling taxes to lower approval tiers is strictly prohibited.

Clause ID: PROC-NO-SPLITTING

Splitting orders, staggering purchase dates, rotating applicant names, or unbundling essential components to circumvent approval thresholds is prohibited. Procurement Management conducts unified reviews of requisitions sharing identical business purposes, close execution timelines, and common suppliers. Where distinct projects require separate purchasing, independent budgets, delivery schedules, and acceptance criteria must be documented.

## III. Emergency Procurement

Clause ID: PROC-EMERGENCY-UPTO-80K

Emergency purchase requisitions up to 80,000 CNY may receive advance approval from the Supply Chain Center Head. Emergency circumstances are strictly limited to events requiring immediate intervention, such as inventory breaching emergency thresholds, critical delivery disruptions, or unforeseen hardware failures; routine planning delays, forgotten requisitions, or commercial pricing convenience do not qualify as emergencies.

Clause ID: PROC-EMERGENCY-RATIFY-2D

Advance-approved emergency purchases must complete formal post-facto ratification within two workdays, submitting emergency justification, transaction value, vendor selection rationale, delivery proof, and preventative remediation plans. Ratification is a mandatory compliance requirement rather than an optional filing; overdue ratifications require freezing unexecuted portions and notifying the Procurement Director and Finance.

Clause ID: PROC-EMERGENCY-OVER-80K

Emergency purchases exceeding 80,000 CNY cannot be advance-approved solely by the Supply Chain Center Head and must secure standard value-tier approvals with expedited priority flags. Requisitions of 200,000 CNY or above continue to require joint approval from the Finance Director and General Manager. Operational urgency modifies processing priority only; it does not waive value-tier controls or order-splitting prohibitions.

## IV. Order Placement and Inbound Acceptance

Clause ID: PROC-PURCHASE-ORDER

Purchase orders must reference approved requisitions, active vendors, material specifications, quantities, unit prices, delivery schedules, acceptance criteria, and breach clauses. Material modifications to price, specifications, or vendor identity post-approval require re-submission; minor logistics changes may be logged with rationale but cannot expand unauthorized commercial commitments.

Clause ID: PROC-ACCEPTANCE

Requesting departments conduct technical acceptance, Warehouse Management verifies physical quantity and packaging integrity, and Procurement Management audits contractual compliance. Non-conforming goods must be quarantined and processed under DOC-PROC-002; marking goods as accepted to meet inventory targets prior to inspection is prohibited. Payment disbursements must strictly match verified inspection records, tax invoices, and purchase orders.

## V. Audit and Exceptions

Clause ID: PROC-AUDIT

Procurement Management conducts monthly audits of vendor active status, approval tier compliance, emergency ratification timelines, order-splitting indicators, and purchase order modifications. Detected unauthorized approvals trigger immediate order suspension and evidence preservation; while operational impact on delivered critical items will be evaluated, downstream business benefits cannot automatically validate non-compliant approvals.

Institutional exceptions require Procurement Management to propose defined scope, validity timeframes, risk evaluations, and compensating controls for formal approval by the Finance Director and General Manager. Exceptions do not apply to individual convenience requests, nor do they alter the codified 50,000 CNY, 200,000 CNY, and 80,000 CNY thresholds. All records must support subsequent factual, accountability, and timeline audits.
