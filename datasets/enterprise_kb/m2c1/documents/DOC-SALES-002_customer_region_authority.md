# DOC-SALES-002 Regional Customer Grading and Sales Authority Rules

Version: 1.0
Scope: Customer master data, regional assignment, customer tier certification, and sales responsibilities for Huaheng Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd.
Responsible Department: Regional Sales Center; Sales Operations Department and General Manager Office supervise execution.

## I. Customer Master Data

These rules ensure that every customer maintains a unified identity across quotations, contracts, customer service, and business statistics. The Regional Sales Center establishes customer master records, capturing stable customer IDs, contracting legal entities, primary operating locations, service regions, customer tiers, effective dates, and account teams. Creating duplicate customer profiles to manipulate discounts, sales quotas, or authority limits is strictly prohibited; tier sharing among corporate affiliates requires explicit written certification.

Clause ID: SALES-CUSTOMER-MASTER

Before creating a new customer profile, search existing names, unified tax/business registration IDs, and historical project logs; potential duplicate matches must be reviewed by Sales Operations. Master profile modifications must preserve prior values, effective dates, and justification reasons; temporary contact names, delivery locations, or single project names cannot substitute for contracting legal entities. Customer tier and regional assignments are governed strictly by active master records.

Clause ID: SALES-CUSTOMER-REGULAR

Regular Customers are defined as accounts that have not met Key or Strategic qualification criteria, or whose elevated tiers have expired or been suspended. A Regular tier designation does not imply lower service quality; rather, it establishes the baseline level for sales authority and resource allocation. Sales teams may request discounts up to the regional base cap but cannot utilize Key Customer percentage point increments or Strategic Customer dedicated approval channels.

Clause ID: SALES-CUSTOMER-KEY

Key Customers must demonstrate continuous procurement potential, defined operational deployment scenarios, and verifiable transaction records, proposed by the Regional Sales Director and confirmed by Sales Operations. Certification must carry explicit validity periods and periodic reviews of payment, purchase, and service history. Key Customer status confers eligibility for up to a two percentage point increment above the regional regular cap, subject to formal approval rather than automatic entitlement.

Clause ID: SALES-CUSTOMER-STRATEGIC

Strategic Customers must offer substantial strategic value to core company markets, product validation programs, or long-term revenue growth, proposed by the Sales Vice President and confirmed by the General Manager Office. Nomination packages must detail collaboration scope, risk assessments, and annual volume commitments. Strategic Customer status enables standard approval workflows up to the fifteen percent discount cap; discounts exceeding fifteen percent require joint approval from both the General Manager and Finance Director.

## II. Regional Assignment

Clause ID: SALES-REGION-EAST

The East China Region manages customers and projects registered within the East China service territory, with base discount caps defined in DOC-SALES-001. Customer delivery destinations located in other regions do not alter regional attribution; evaluations must consider contracting legal entity, decision-making headquarters, primary service responsibility, and historical master data to prevent regional shopping for higher discount caps.

Clause ID: SALES-REGION-SOUTH

South China customers are maintained by the South China account team. For cross-regional enterprise groups, the South China team may participate in local fulfillment, but primary territory ownership is designated centrally by the Regional Sales Center. Regular customers in South China are subject to a six percent base cap; Key Customer increments require approval from the Regional Sales Director.

Clause ID: SALES-REGION-NORTH

The North China Region handles sales forecasting, quotation coordination, and customer service liaison for its assigned accounts. In the event of territory overlaps, duplicate quotes or conflicting tier approaches to the same customer are prohibited; conflicting quotes must be frozen, and the Regional Sales Center determines lead and collaborating roles within two workdays based on master records and project facts.

Clause ID: SALES-REGION-WEST

The West China Region manages its designated accounts; cross-provincial fulfillment and remote service costs must be reflected transparently in project financial models. The West China regular customer nine percent cap is not a default loophole for cross-regional projects; if primary decision-making and service responsibilities reside elsewhere, accounts cannot be transferred into West China solely based on final installation coordinates.

## III. Authority and Collaboration

Clause ID: SALES-CROSS-REGION

Cross-regional enterprise customers must be assigned a lead region and designated supporting regions. The lead region owns master profile maintenance, unified quotations, and contract alignment, while supporting regions handle local relationships, delivery, and field service. Commission and credit allocation must be agreed upon prior to quotation submission; delaying shipment or disputing approved customer pricing over territory rights post-contract is strictly prohibited.

Clause ID: SALES-AUTHORITY-BOUNDARY

Regional teams are authorized to initiate quotation requests within the boundaries of their territory and customer tier, but final discount approvals remain strictly governed by the approval roles specified in DOC-SALES-001. Customer tier certification, discount approval, and contract signing represent distinct authorities; holding one authority does not permit bypassing or substituting for another.

Clause ID: SALES-CUSTOMER-REVIEW

Regular, Key, and Strategic customer accounts undergo formal review at least annually; early reviews are triggered by severe payment delinquencies, fictitious project declarations, major compliance breaches, or extended inactivity. Tier downgrades or suspensions must state effective dates and treatment of unexecuted orders, without retroactively invalidating legally executed contracts or concealing risks to maintain high tier ratings.

## IV. Exceptions and Data Quality

Clause ID: SALES-CUSTOMER-EXCEPTION

When centralized group procurement requires temporary price or service sharing, Sales Operations compiles an authorized list of group entities, applicable regions, and validity dates, subject to Sales VP approval. This exception does not automatically elevate all affiliates to Strategic status, nor does it override established pricing authority; discounts exceeding fifteen percent continue to require joint executive approval.

Clause ID: SALES-CUSTOMER-AUDIT

Sales Operations performs monthly audits covering duplicate profiles, frequent territory shifts, expired tiers, multi-channel conflicting quotes, and discount authority compliance. Detected anomalies trigger immediate freezing of master profile updates and notification to responsible account teams. Master data deduplication must preserve historical order and approval linkages; deleting records to fabricate artificial uniformity or higher tier ratings is prohibited.

## V. Execution and Appeals

Territory or customer tier disputes are first reviewed by the Regional Sales Center based on factual evidence; unresolved disputes escalate to the Sales Vice President for final determination. Ongoing appeals do not impede the execution of existing valid contracts, but new unauthorized quotations must be suspended. All tier certifications and modifications must trace back to application files, designated approvers, and effective dates, ensuring all subsequent pricing, service, and analytics reference a single consistent business identity.
