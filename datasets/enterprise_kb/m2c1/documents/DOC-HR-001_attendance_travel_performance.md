# DOC-HR-001 Employee Attendance, Business Travel, and Performance Management Policy

Version: 1.0
Scope: Employee attendance tracking, domestic and international business travel, and performance evaluation reviews for Huaheng Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd.
Responsible Department: Human Resources Department; Department Heads and Finance Management Department coordinate in execution.

## I. Attendance Management

Employees must record working hours, leaves, and out-of-office business trips truthfully based on role assignments; punching timecards on behalf of others, fabricating attendance, or concealing absences is strictly prohibited. Flexible or remote work arrangements require formal confirmation of scope, schedule, and deliverables by the Department Head. HR maintains institutional policy standards and exception workflows, while Department Heads verify operational business facts; neither can substitute for the other.

Clause ID: HR-ATTENDANCE-RECORD

Attendance records must originate from authorized company systems, capturing date, status, and necessary justifications. During system outages, employees submit objective evidence upon system recovery, which Department Heads verify prior to correction; corrections must retain original logs and justification reasons. Coercing employees to record leaves as official travel or deleting tardiness records to manipulate departmental metrics is prohibited.

Clause ID: HR-ATTENDANCE-EXCEPTION

When business travel, customer on-site visits, training, or approved remote work prevents standard timecard punching, the corresponding status code must be selected and linked to approved authorizations. Temporary arrangements require advance approval where feasible, or post-facto explanation within one workday during emergencies. Attendance exceptions do not automatically confer expense reimbursement entitlements.

Clause ID: HR-LEAVE-PROCESS

Leave applications must specify leave category, timeframes, and work handover arrangements, submitted through authorized workflow approval channels. In medical emergencies or urgent family crises, employees may notify supervisors first and submit system filings upon recovery; personal health or privacy documentation may be accessed solely by authorized roles. Departments shall not demand disclosure of personal sensitive information beyond necessary verification scope.

## II. Domestic Business Travel

Clause ID: HR-DOMESTIC-ADVANCE-2D

Domestic business travel applications must, in principle, be submitted two workdays in advance, specifying trip purpose, destination, start/end dates, transportation and accommodation budget, and operational handover. The two-workday requirement represents advance submission lead time rather than a guarantee of approval completion; applicants must allow sufficient review time. Routine meetings or predictable customer visits must not repeatedly utilize emergency approval channels.

Clause ID: HR-DOMESTIC-EMERGENCY

Urgent domestic business trips may depart in advance, provided post-facto ratification is completed within one workday following return. Emergency justifications are strictly limited to critical on-site customer emergencies, supply disruptions, or other unforeseen events where delays would inflict significant operational loss. Ratification filings must include complete itinerary, actual expenditures, and managerial endorsement; post-facto ratification cannot become routine practice.

Clause ID: HR-TRAVEL-CHANGE

When approved itineraries experience major alterations in dates, destinations, or budgets, formal change requests must be submitted promptly. Minor objective variances such as carrier delays can be explained with receipts during reimbursement; adding new cities, multi-day extensions, or significant budget increases require re-approval. Extra expenses arising from personal choices shall not be borne by the company.

## III. Overseas Business Travel

Clause ID: HR-OVERSEAS-ADVANCE-10D

Overseas business travel applications must, in principle, be submitted ten workdays in advance to accommodate visa processing, international travel insurance, risk assessments, and secure data access preparations. Applications must detail target country/region, host partner entities, portable hardware devices, data sensitivity levels, and designated emergency contact roles. The ten-workday timeline cannot be conflated with the domestic two-workday rule, nor does pre-purchasing tickets confer automatic approval.

Clause ID: HR-OVERSEAS-SECURITY

Before taking devices containing L3 or L4 data abroad, the Information Security Office must evaluate minimum necessary data scope, full-disk encryption, secure remote access controls, and device loss remediation protocols. When security controls cannot be verified, dedicated clean devices must be issued or non-essential data purged. Travel approval does not replace data access authorization governed by DOC-SEC-001.

Clause ID: HR-TRAVEL-EXPENSE

Travel expenses are reimbursed against approved budgets and company standards, ensuring receipts, itinerary logs, and business objectives match. Excess spending or budget overruns are processed under DOC-FIN-001; travel application approval does not automatically establish expense compliance. Shared transportation or lodging expenses must be apportioned transparently to prevent duplicate claims against identical invoices.

## IV. Performance Review and Appeals

Clause ID: HR-PERFORMANCE-PUBLISH

Upon releasing performance appraisal results, employees must be informed of the evaluation cycle, primary metrics, final ratings, and feedback/appeal mechanisms. Supervisors evaluate employees based on objective deliverables and documented behavioral records, excluding factors unrelated to job responsibilities. The formal publication date is logged by the system as the baseline start date for appeal windows and cannot be backdated arbitrarily to deny appeals.

Clause ID: HR-PERFORMANCE-APPEAL-5D

Performance review appeals must be filed within five workdays following official result publication, detailing disputed metrics, factual evidence, and requested audit points. Submissions within the fifth workday are considered timely and valid. Upon validating application completeness, HR assigns an independent reviewer other than the original evaluating manager; submitting an appeal shall not serve as grounds for retaliatory downgrades in subsequent evaluation cycles.

Clause ID: HR-PERFORMANCE-REVIEW

Performance appeals audit objective goal versions, measurement data baselines, work artifacts, scoring consistency, and approval chains, with authority to sustain, adjust, or mandate re-evaluation. Final determinations must document facts and rationale, preserving original ratings and adjustment histories. An appeal is not a renegotiation of original performance targets, nor does it accept generalized assertions unrelated to the evaluation period.

## V. Data Privacy and Governance

Clause ID: HR-RECORD-ACCESS

Attendance, business travel, and performance records are accessed strictly under role-based least-privilege principles. Department Heads view data within their management scope, HR maintains master employee records, and Finance views travel details solely for expense audit verification. Exporting or sharing records containing personally identifiable information requires explicit business justification and validity timeframes, preventing uncontrolled data sprawl.

HR conducts monthly audits of attendance irregularities and travel ratifications, alongside periodic reviews of performance appeal timelines and outcome consistency. Where policy does not specify granular flight cabin classes, hotel city tier caps, or annual bonus multipliers, fabricating ungrounded answers is prohibited; such matters are governed by separate active company standards. All modifications and exceptions must trace back to verifiable facts, approving authorities, and execution timestamps.
