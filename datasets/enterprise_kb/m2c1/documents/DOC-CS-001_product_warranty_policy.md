# DOC-CS-001 Product Warranty and After-Sales Service Management Policy

Version: 1.0
Scope: Inspection terminals, original batteries, and charging docks sold by Huaheng Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd.
Responsible Department: Customer Service Department; Supply Chain Center and Sales Operations Department assist in execution.

## I. General Provisions and Responsibilities

This policy establishes unified standards for warranty periods, liability determinations, exception handling, and after-sales service workflows. The Customer Service Department is responsible for intake, evidence collection, and customer communication. The Supply Chain Center handles spare parts and factory return logistics. The Sales Operations Department is strictly prohibited from making verbal warranty commitments beyond contractual terms. All warranty periods commence from the date indicated on the valid proof of delivery; if proof of delivery is missing, the later of verifiable warehouse dispatch or customer receipt records shall prevail.

Clause ID: CS-WARRANTY-SCOPE

Warranty coverage applies exclusively to verifiable serial-numbered Product A, Product B, Model A Original Battery, Model B Original Battery, Standard Charging Dock, and Industrial Rugged Dock sold by Huaheng Intelligent. Intake personnel must verify product model, accessory identity, purchase channel, and delivery date. Applying host terminal warranty periods to batteries or charging docks, or substituting terms between different accessory models, is strictly prohibited.

Clause ID: CS-WARRANTY-RESPONSIBILITY

Upon receipt of complete documentation, the Customer Service Department creates a service ticket detailing symptoms, operating environment, firmware version, and physical condition. Disassembly for diagnostic evaluation requires explicit customer authorization and photographic evidence. When batch quality defects are identified, Customer Service must notify Quality and Supply Chain teams immediately; standard individual cases shall not delay committed response times due to internal review.

## II. Product A Warranty Rules

Clause ID: CS-WARRANTY-A-DEVICE

The standard base warranty period for Product A host terminal is twenty-four months, applicable to standard factory configurations operating within normal temperature limits, charged according to user manuals, and without unauthorized disassembly. The host scope covers internal control boards, display units, and communication modules within the housing, excluding detachable batteries and charging docks. Replacement of the host terminal does not automatically extend warranty periods for other accessories in the original order.

Clause ID: CS-WARRANTY-A-BATTERY

The base warranty period for Model A Original Battery is twelve months, provided the battery is used with Product A, charged using original factory equipment, and free from crushing, dropping, liquid ingress, or unauthorized modification. Natural capacity degradation does not constitute a manufacturing defect; if testing confirms cell abnormality under warranty conditions, Customer Service will arrange a replacement of identical specification.

Clause ID: CS-WARRANTY-STANDARD-DOCK

The base warranty period for Standard Charging Dock is eighteen months. Coverage applies to manufacturing defects in interfaces, indicator lights, and control modules under normal power supply conditions. Damage caused by non-original power adapters, incorrect voltage, liquid ingress, or external force is excluded. Charging docks and host devices are registered separately and do not share warranty durations despite belonging to the same purchase order.

## III. Product B Warranty Rules

Clause ID: CS-WARRANTY-B-DEVICE

The standard base warranty period for Product B host terminal is thirty-six months, applicable to factory standard configurations of rugged inspection terminals. In dusty or humid environments, users must ensure protective port covers remain closed and properly maintained. Damage resulting from exceeding operating temperature thresholds or compromising enclosure seals does not constitute a manufacturing defect; rugged product classification alone does not warrant claims outside operating limits.

Clause ID: CS-WARRANTY-B-BATTERY

The base warranty period for Model B Original Battery is eighteen months, applicable exclusively to verifiable serial-numbered genuine components. Applying Product B host terminal's thirty-six-month period or Model A battery's twelve-month period to Model B batteries is strictly prohibited. Technical inspection must distinguish abnormal self-discharge, cyclic degradation, and external physical damage, documenting diagnostic rationale in the final determination report.

Clause ID: CS-WARRANTY-INDUSTRIAL-DOCK

The base warranty period for Industrial Rugged Dock is twenty-four months. Its rugged design does not waive requirements for proper electrical grounding, specified operating voltage, and dry maintenance. Failures resulting from power surges, unauthorized wiring modifications, or non-genuine charging equipment are excluded from warranty coverage. Eligible control module failures will be repaired or replaced accordingly.

## IV. Exclusions, Workflows, and Exceptions

Clause ID: CS-WARRANTY-EXCLUSIONS

Liquid ingress, crushing or impact damage, unauthorized disassembly, use of non-genuine charging equipment, exceeding operating temperature limits, and unauthorized firmware modification are uniform warranty exclusions. Exclusion determinations must be substantiated by diagnostic logs, inspection reports, or verifiable physical evidence rather than subjective assumptions; when multiple contributing causes exist, their respective relationships to the failure must be documented individually.

Clause ID: CS-WARRANTY-FIRMWARE

Installation of officially released firmware via authorized company channels is not considered unauthorized modification. Using unauthorized custom ROMs, bypassing signature verification, or altering low-level system parameters voids base warranty coverage. If a hardware defect has no causal relationship with firmware alterations, Customer Service must submit the case for technical re-evaluation rather than categorically denying service based solely on modification traces.

Clause ID: CS-WARRANTY-PROCESS

The intake workflow proceeds sequentially: identity verification, documentation intake, remote troubleshooting, shipment or on-site testing, liability determination, repair or replacement, and customer confirmation. Customer Service tracks each stage under a unified service ticket ID; when supplier analysis is required, Customer Service remains the single point of contact for the customer and shall not require customers to submit documentation repeatedly across multiple departments.

Clause ID: CS-WARRANTY-EXCEPTION-APPROVAL

Goodwill customer service provided outside standard warranty terms does not alter institutional policy. In cases of major customer impact or batch quality risks, Customer Service may draft a case summary, cost estimate, and proposed solution for joint review by Sales Operations and Finance. Decisions involving public policy alterations or substantial financial compensation must be submitted to the General Manager Office for formal approval with explicit one-time exception boundaries.

## V. Records and Compliance

All after-sales service records must be linked to product serial numbers, order IDs, inspection reports, liability determinations, and resolution outcomes. Deleting adverse evidence or misclassifying chargeable repairs as warranty coverage is strictly prohibited. Where an authorized contract provides superior terms, the verifiable contract terms prevail; areas not addressed in the contract remain governed by this policy. Customer Service audits quarterly warranty period compliance across products and accessories to correct any confusion between host devices, batteries, and charging docks.
