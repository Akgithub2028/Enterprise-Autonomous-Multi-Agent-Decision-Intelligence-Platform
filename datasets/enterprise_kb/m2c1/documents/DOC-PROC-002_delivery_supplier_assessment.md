# DOC-PROC-002 Procurement Delivery Exception and Supplier Assessment Policy

Version: 1.0
Scope: Supplier delivery monitoring, exception remediation, and quarterly performance evaluation for Huaheng Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd.
Responsible Department: Supply Chain Center; Procurement Management Department, Warehouse Management Department, and Quality roles coordinate in execution.

## I. Delivery Tracking

The Supply Chain Center tracks committed delivery dates, actual inbound receipts, quantities, acceptance results, delivery exceptions, and attribution liabilities by purchase order. Supplier evaluations utilize verifiable order and inspection data rather than subjective impressions. Xingyuan Power, Ruihe Precision, and Yuntong Supply Chain are evaluated under identical standardized criteria. Delays caused by authorized internal company scheduling adjustments constitute evaluation exceptions, must be flagged separately, and cannot be attributed as supplier defaults.

Clause ID: PROC-DELIVERY-BASELINE

Delivery baselines are established based on mutually confirmed purchase order dates and quantities. When authorized modifications occur, original baselines, modification justifications, and updated commitments are preserved; unauthorized verbal adjustments do not modify performance baselines. Partial shipments are evaluated per individual batch commitment; early deliveries creating warehouse congestion risks must also be logged rather than focusing solely on final order completion.

Clause ID: PROC-DELIVERY-EXCEPTION

When an impending delivery failure is anticipated, the supplier must notify the company promptly regarding root causes, affected quantities, recovery timelines, and mitigation proposals. The Supply Chain Center assesses safety stock thresholds and customer order commitments, activating alternative sourcing or sales allocation controls when necessary. Exception ticket closure requires physical inbound acceptance and corrective evidence rather than verbal promises.

Clause ID: PROC-DELIVERY-ROOT-CAUSE

Major or recurring delivery exceptions require 5-Why root-cause analysis across personnel, equipment, materials, processes, and external factors, differentiating random carrier delays, production capacity constraints, quality rework, and internal company scope changes. Corrective action plans must specify owners, deadlines, and verification metrics; when identical failures recur, Procurement Management must escalate the vendor risk rating rather than accepting repetitive generic explanations.

## II. Scoring Methodology and Performance Tiers

Clause ID: PROC-SCORE-METHOD

Comprehensive performance scores are calculated across defined dimensions including quality acceptance, on-time delivery (OTD), collaborative responsiveness, and commercial compliance, structured on a 100-point scale. Every deduction must link to verifiable purchase order or incident evidence; duplicate deductions across multiple dimensions for a single incident without independent rationale are prohibited. Quarterly performance results are calculated by the Supply Chain Center, audited by Procurement Management, and formally communicated to suppliers.

Clause ID: PROC-SCORE-PREFERRED-85

A comprehensive score of eighty-five points and above earns a Preferred rating. A Preferred rating indicates prioritized consideration for quote solicitations and partnership opportunities under equal conditions; it does not guarantee fixed order allocations, nor does it waive transaction-level approvals or receiving inspections. Achieving exactly eighty-five points qualifies for Preferred status; subsequent major quality incidents may trigger interim risk controls.

Clause ID: PROC-SCORE-QUALIFIED-70-84

A comprehensive score from seventy to eighty-four points earns a Qualified rating. A score of exactly seventy points is Qualified; eighty-four points does not qualify for Preferred. Qualified suppliers participate in standard procurement but must submit improvement action items addressing major deduction categories; rounding up eighty-four points to eighty-five to maintain artificial Preferred status is strictly prohibited.

Clause ID: PROC-SCORE-RECTIFY-60-69

A comprehensive score from sixty to sixty-nine points places the supplier in Rectification status with mandatory remediation deadlines. A supplier scoring sixty-eight points must submit root cause analysis, action items, designated owners, and completion dates; Procurement Management may restrict new product category additions or order volumes. Suppliers cannot be represented as Qualified prior to passing rectification verification; when critical supply continuity mandates continued ordering, documented risk assessments and enhanced controls are required.

Clause ID: PROC-SCORE-SUSPEND-BELOW60

A comprehensive score below sixty points results in immediate Suspension of new purchase orders. Suspension does not automatically cancel existing executed orders; Procurement Management evaluates in-transit shipments, alternative sourcing options, and default liabilities, applying enhanced quality inspection for continuing orders. Restoring order eligibility requires verified remediation completion and formal re-approval, rather than relying on passive score recovery over time.

## III. Special On-Time Delivery (OTD) Rules

Clause ID: PROC-OTD-BELOW90-TWO-QUARTERS

Suppliers whose on-time delivery rate falls below ninety percent for two consecutive quarters are subject to mandatory Special Rectification. This threshold strictly requires "two consecutive quarters" where each individual quarter falls below ninety percent; a single sub-90% quarter or a quarter reaching exactly ninety percent does not trigger this clause directly, though it impacts overall scores and general improvement actions.

Clause ID: PROC-OTD-CALCULATION

The on-time delivery rate is calculated as the ratio of on-time, quality-acceptable order batches to total scheduled delivery batches. Inbound shipments rescheduled via approved internal modifications are evaluated against revised dates; supplier-initiated unapproved delays are evaluated against original baselines. Quarters with zero scheduled shipments are flagged as no-sample periods rather than fabricated as 100%.

Clause ID: PROC-SPECIAL-RECTIFICATION

Special Rectification programs must incorporate delivery root-cause analysis, capacity and material planning, milestone targets, weekly tracking cadences, and exit criteria. The Supply Chain Center verifies continuous performance recovery, and Procurement Management determines whether restrictions can be lifted. If inventory breaches emergency lines during rectification, alternative suppliers may be engaged in parallel, subject to the value-tier and ratification rules in DOC-PROC-001.

## IV. Disputes and Supplier Offboarding

Clause ID: PROC-SCORE-APPEAL

Suppliers may submit a formal appeal within five workdays of receiving quarterly scorecards, supported by purchase orders, delivery receipts, or change records. Unsubstantiated verbal objections are not accepted; original scoring personnel provide calculation breakdowns, and Procurement Management assigns an independent auditor to review the appeal. Adjustments must preserve original scores and revision justifications.

Clause ID: PROC-SUPPLIER-EXIT

Persistent rectification failures, severe quality risks, or commercial compliance violations trigger formal offboarding assessments. Prior to offboarding, open orders, tooling, technical documentation, business data, and alternative supply availability must be reconciled to prevent disruption of critical deliveries. For suppliers with data access, permissions must be revoked immediately per DOC-SEC-001 with audit logs preserved.

## V. Reporting and Governance

Quarterly performance reports must detail composite scores, rating tiers, on-time delivery rates, major delivery exceptions, rectification statuses, and evidence citations, avoiding ungrounded conclusions. Supply Chain is prohibited from omitting late shipments to preserve vendor relationships, and Procurement Management is prohibited from imposing arbitrary deductions for price leverage. All rating boundaries are evaluated against raw scores without ungrounded rounding that alters classification tiers.
