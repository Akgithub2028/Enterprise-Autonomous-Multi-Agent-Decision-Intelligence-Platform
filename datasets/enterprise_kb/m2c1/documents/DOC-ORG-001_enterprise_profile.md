# DOC-ORG-001 Enterprise Profile and Demo Scope Description

Version: 1.0
Scope: Enterprise identity, operational domain, demonstration documentation scope, and business data criteria for Huaheng Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd.
Responsible Department: General Manager Office; Warehouse Management Department, Procurement Management Department, Sales Operations Department, Customer Service Department, and Information Security Office coordinate in maintenance.

## I. Enterprise Identity and Nature

Clause ID: ENT-IDENTITY

Huaheng Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd. serves as the unified enterprise identity for this AI Agent platform project, maintaining consistency across institutional policies, product designations, operational scenarios, and system answers. Users may refer to the entity as Huaheng Intelligent, Huaheng Tech, the Company, our company, this enterprise, or the sample enterprise; these references point exclusively to this controlled demonstration dataset and cannot be used to extrapolate external corporate entities, registered addresses, employee rosters, customer lists, or commercial licenses. This identity serves solely for demonstration indexing and query understanding within the controlled dataset boundaries.

Clause ID: ENT-FICTIONAL-DEMO

Huaheng Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd. is a synthetic demonstration enterprise and does not represent real-world entities of identical or similar names. All policies, product lines, suppliers, metrics, and business descriptions in this knowledge base are designed for benchmarking and demonstration purposes. They cannot be used as real-world corporate policies, contractual commitments, purchase orders, technical specifications, commercial pricing, or legal advice. When queries require real-world external verification, the system must state that current internal materials do not provide external grounding.

Clause ID: ENT-INDUSTRY

The enterprise operational baseline is situated within smart device manufacturing and operations. Documentation focuses on business management workflows surrounding smart inspection terminals and associated accessories, rather than exhaustive engineering schematics, factory line throughputs, patent portfolios, market shares, financial filings, or industry rankings. Industry descriptions serve to ground the context of internal policies and operational data queries.

Clause ID: ENT-PRODUCTS

Demonstration product lines comprise Product A, Product B, and their respective genuine accessories: Model A Original Battery and Model B Original Battery; customer service documentation also covers Standard Charging Docks and Industrial Rugged Docks. Product A and Product B represent stable product identifiers and must not be extrapolated into unregistered product variants, technical parameters, or unauthorized commercial offers. Host terminals and genuine batteries must be differentiated across inventory, warranty, and replacement workflows.

Clause ID: ENT-BUSINESS-LIFECYCLE

Core business workflows include supplier onboarding, procurement authorization, inventory monitoring and replenishment, sales discount governance, regional fulfillment, warranty service, repair intake, and cross-functional operational analysis. Inventory shortages trigger risk evaluations combining replenishment schedules, pending deliveries, and warranty replacement buffers; this analytical capability does not imply that the Agent can autonomously issue purchase orders, reallocate stock, or alter database records.

Clause ID: ENT-DEPARTMENTS

Warehouse Management manages inventory accounting, cycle counting, and safety stock monitoring; the Supply Chain Center tracks demand trends, arrival schedules, and replenishment planning; Procurement Management oversees supplier onboarding and purchase requisitions; Sales Operations evaluates order commitments and delivery impact; Customer Service manages warranty, DOA exchanges, and repair intake. Departmental responsibilities adhere strictly to codified institutional policies; overview summaries cannot override specific policy thresholds, approval criteria, or exception handling.

Clause ID: ENT-KB-COVERAGE

The active knowledge base incorporates institutional policy documentation spanning customer service, sales, inventory, procurement, finance, human resources, and security governance, preserving explicit clause lineages. Knowledge QA answers must ground strictly in retrievable documentation and attach verifiable citation markers; where multiple policies intersect, the scope of referenced materials must be cited explicitly rather than relying on generalized summaries.

Clause ID: ENT-DATA-COVERAGE

Operational business data encompasses inventory threshold alerts, procurement requisitions, authorized relational tables, and joint knowledge-data decision analysis. Data query answers must rely strictly on output returned by connected, guarded data sources and authorized schemas; unintegrated systems, unrecorded historical logs, or missing fields cannot be fabricated via extrapolation, nor can static knowledge policy text be mischaracterized as real-time operational data.

Clause ID: ENT-EXCLUSIONS

Current documentation explicitly excludes open-web information, real-time news feeds, unlisted product models, real customer or employee personal profiles, unintegrated ERP transaction logs, complete manufacturing blueprints, arbitrary legal determinations, or external market intelligence. For queries exceeding these document and database boundaries, the Agent must state lack of evidence or reject the unsupported request rather than fabricating answers, citations, or metrics.

Clause ID: ENT-DATA-STATUS

The demonstration dataset version is 1.0, with an institutional policy effective date standardized at 2026-01-01. Versioning and timestamps define the maintenance baseline for this demonstration package and do not reflect real-world publication dates or live production inventory clocks. Users should interpret answers within the context of the controlled documentation and independently verify findings against live enterprise production systems.

Documentation maintenance requires strict consistency across corporate names, product identifiers, departmental roles, and clause IDs. Overview summaries shall not modify specific thresholds, procurement tiers, warranty durations, or data access controls. Detailed queries must retrieve corresponding policy or relational evidence; unrecorded facts cannot be assumed based on naming similarity.
