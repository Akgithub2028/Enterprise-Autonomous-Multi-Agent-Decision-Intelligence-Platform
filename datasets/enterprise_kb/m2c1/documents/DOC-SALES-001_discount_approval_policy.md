# DOC-SALES-001 Sales Discount and Special Pricing Approval Policy

Version: 1.0
Scope: Quotations, discounts, and special pricing for products and standard accessories sold by Huaheng Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd.
Responsible Department: Sales Operations Department; Regional Sales Center, Finance Management Department, and General Manager Office coordinate in execution.

## I. Price Discipline

This policy uses the official active company price list as the baseline for all discount calculations. Sales personnel must submit quotation requests only after verifying customer identity, geographical region, customer tier, and product scope. Making commitments prior to approval or circumventing authority thresholds via free gifts, service waivers, split orders, or underpriced accessories is strictly prohibited. Discount percentages are calculated against tax-inclusive standard list prices; where unified framework contracts exist, their applicable scope must be verified first.

Clause ID: SALES-DISCOUNT-BASIS

Quotation requests must contain a stable customer ID, customer tier, contracting region, product items and quantities, standard list price, requested price, discount rate, validity period, and commercial rationale. Sales Operations verifies calculation consistency and historical commitments; Finance reviews gross margins and budget impact where required. Incomplete requests must be returned for revision; verbal consents cannot replace system approval records.

Clause ID: SALES-DISCOUNT-EAST-REGULAR

The regional base discount cap for regular customers in East China is eight percent. A cap represents the maximum allowable threshold under standard authority and does not imply automatic entitlement for every transaction. Applicants must justify order quantity, competitive context, and payment terms; cross-regional shipping addresses alone cannot alter customer regional assignment to access higher discount caps.

Clause ID: SALES-DISCOUNT-SOUTH-REGULAR

The regional base discount cap for regular customers in South China is six percent. This rule cannot be conflated with the eight percent East China cap or Key Customer increments. When customer tier confirmation is pending, requests must be processed under regular customer rules; quoting Key Customer rates ahead of tier confirmation and attempting retroactive ratification is strictly prohibited.

Clause ID: SALES-DISCOUNT-NORTH-REGULAR

The regional base discount cap for regular customers in North China is seven percent. If an order spans multiple regions, calculations must be partitioned by actual customer entity location and contractual responsibility, rather than uniformly applying the highest regional cap. Sales Operations resolves regional attribution disputes according to customer master data and service territory assignments in DOC-SALES-002.

Clause ID: SALES-DISCOUNT-WEST-REGULAR

The regional base discount cap for regular customers in West China is nine percent. This higher cap accommodates local fulfillment and market conditions but does not waive gross margin reviews, payment terms, or export compliance rules. Any scheme attempting to resell equipment into other regions via fictitious West China projects constitutes an evasion of authority and requires immediate quotation freeze and review.

## II. Customer Tier Authorization

Clause ID: SALES-DISCOUNT-KEY-PLUS2

Key Customers may receive up to an additional two percentage points above their regional regular customer discount cap, requiring approval from the Regional Sales Director. For example, a South China Key Customer standard cap is eight percent, not the fifteen percent Strategic Customer rate. Incremental points are valid only during active Key Customer tier status; if status expires or is suspended, regular customer caps apply.

Clause ID: SALES-DISCOUNT-STRATEGIC-15

The standard maximum discount cap for Strategic Customers is fifteen percent, requiring approval from the Sales Vice President. This cap is not cumulative with regional base caps or Key Customer increments. Strategic Customer orders must verify contractual legal entity, product scope, payment schedule, and quote validity. Affiliates not explicitly registered in the Strategic Customer master roster cannot inherit tier status automatically.

Clause ID: SALES-DISCOUNT-OVER15

Discounts exceeding fifteen percent fall outside standard authority and require joint approval from both the General Manager and the Finance Director. Application dossiers must specify minimum acceptable price, gross margin impact, customer commitments, competitive evidence, and exit clauses. Consent from a single approver is insufficient; the quotation system permits formal document generation only after both required approvals are recorded.

## III. Special Pricing Controls

Clause ID: SALES-DISCOUNT-BUNDLE

Bundled quotations covering host terminals, batteries, and charging docks must itemize standard list prices and discounts separately before computing aggregate order discount. Maintaining nominal discounts on Product A hosts while drastically underpricing Model A batteries to circumvent authorization limits is strictly prohibited; quantifiable value in free trials, extended warranties, or shipping fee waivers must be incorporated into the commercial concession evaluation.

Clause ID: SALES-DISCOUNT-VALIDITY

Approved pricing applies strictly to the customer, products, quantity ranges, and validity windows specified in the application. Decreased quantities, deteriorated payment terms, changed legal entities, or expired quotes require re-approval. Approval records cannot be transferred to another customer or region; repeat orders matching prior conditions must still verify active customer tier standing and current price list validity.

Clause ID: SALES-DISCOUNT-EMERGENCY

Urgent bidding deadlines do not alter discount caps or approval authorities. Applicants may flag requests as urgent with explicit deadline timestamps for prioritized review; if complete approvals cannot be secured within the required window, only quotations within existing pre-authorized limits may be submitted. Post-hoc ratification cannot validate unauthorized special pricing.

## IV. Audit and Exceptions

Clause ID: SALES-DISCOUNT-AUDIT

Sales Operations conducts monthly audits reconciling approved discounts, contract pricing, invoiced amounts, gift items, and credit terms to detect order-splitting and cross-regional arbitrage. When discrepancies are discovered, unexecuted portions are suspended pending explanation, and Finance assesses revenue and margin impact. Corrections must preserve original quotes, justifications, approvers, and timestamps without overwriting audit history.

Clause ID: SALES-DISCOUNT-EXCEPTION

Government-mandated prices, legally binding framework procurement awards, or company-wide promotional campaigns require Sales Operations to establish pre-defined product lists, validity terms, and accounting methodologies. Orders within the approved list execute under the special scheme; transactions outside the list revert to this standard policy. Special campaigns shall not imply permanent modifications to base authorizations for Regular, Key, or Strategic customers.

## V. Execution Workflow

Following submission, the Regional Sales Director verifies customer and project details, Sales Operations checks policy rules, designated approvers review thresholds, and the quotation system generates a version-controlled formal quote. Rejections and returns must state clear reasons. All pricing commitments across all channels must trace back to approved system records; commitments lacking approval records cannot proceed to contract signing or product shipment.
